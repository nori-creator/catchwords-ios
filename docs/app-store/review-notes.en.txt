CatchWords helps people learn Taiwanese Mandarin (Traditional Chinese) words from photos of everyday things. Free, no in-app purchases. New in 1.0.4: an optional rewarded video ad (Google AdMob), described under ADS below.

SIGN IN
Use the demo account in "Sign-in information" above: on the first screen, tap "Sign in with email", enter the email and password, and tap "Sign in". The account has finished the first-run setup and the AI consent, already has words, and shows the app in English (Settings > Language > Your language changes it). On a new device the consent screen "Sending data to AI" may appear once; tap "Agree and start". Sign in with Apple and Google are also offered on the first screen; "New here? Sign up" creates an account with email.

MAIN FLOW
1. Camera tab: photograph any object, or pick a photo with the Photos button. Word tags appear on the objects after a few seconds.
2. Tap a word: its sticker (cut out of the photo), the word and its pronunciation appear, and the word is added to the collection. A word that is not shown can be looked up with "Type a different word".
3. Collection tab: tap a word for zhuyin, pinyin, meaning, pronunciation (speaker button) and example sentences.
4. Review tab: 4-choice questions with the user's own photos.
5. Settings: languages, level, notifications, Legal (Terms, Privacy Policy, consent to send data to AI), Sign out, and Delete account at the bottom (deletes the account and its data on our server).

DATA AND AI
Photos and typed words go only to our server (our own Supabase project; website catchwords.app). Our server sends them to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini, with Anthropic Claude as a backup; the operator may switch a feature to another provider named in the privacy policy) only after the user agrees on the in-app screen "Sending data to AI", shown before the first AI feature. Without consent nothing is sent and the camera stays off; collected words and review still work. Consent can be withdrawn in Settings > Legal. Pronunciation of Taiwanese Mandarin: Microsoft Azure AI Speech (text only). Optional replacement photos for a word: Unsplash / Wikimedia Commons, with credit.

ADS (NEW IN 1.0.4)
- The free plan allows a number of catches (photo -> word added) per day: 10 a day in an account's first week, then 5 a day. Reviews, the collection and every other screen are never limited.
- After the day's free catches, the sheet "That's all your catches for today" offers "Watch an ad for 1 more". Only if the user taps it, a Google AdMob rewarded video plays; watching it to the end adds one catch for that day. Ads are never shown at launch, during a catch, or on their own.
- To see it with the demo account: catch 5 words in the Camera tab (the account is older than a week), then try a sixth. If AdMob has no ad to serve at that moment, the sheet says "No ad is available right now" and nothing else changes.
- No App Tracking Transparency prompt: the app does not track and does not use the IDFA. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) is shown before the first ad; the choice can be changed in Settings > Legal > Ad privacy choices.

WHY SIGN-IN IS REQUIRED
Photos, collected words and review history are saved per account on our server, and the AI features have a per-account daily limit to prevent abuse, so almost nothing works without an account.

OTHER
- Camera, photo library, location and notification permissions are optional and asked only when the feature is used.
- Some diagnostic screens in Settings are shown only to the developer's own account; they do not appear for other users or the demo account.
- Every account learns Taiwanese Mandarin. The interface can be shown in English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Korean or Vietnamese (Settings > Language); the sign-in screens follow the device language.
- The app works the same in every territory where it is offered.
