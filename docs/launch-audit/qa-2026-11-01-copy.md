## Not in strings.json (point 4)
- PlaceEditSheet:110 `Button("OK")` was already reported. The other bare literals (CatchWords, PRO, NEW, STEP n, Lv.n, "{n} m", "0.7×", percentages and counts) are common notation and are fine.
- `Resources/Onboarding/sample_word.json` holds the tutorial sample's text in 6 languages and is not covered by check_l10n.py. The ko issues are above. In the en/es/ko/vi "taiwan" lines, the bolded Chinese terms use the Japanese middle dot 「半糖・少糖・無糖」 (zh-TW correctly uses 、).
- es InfoPlist names the setting differently from Settings (above). ko/zh/en/vi InfoPlist texts match their Settings labels.
- Dead keys: 208 keys are never used as literals. Some are server sentences matched through readerSafe; those translations are fine. Others are stale. For example, 「月」 is translated as "Month" in en but as the weekday "Lun"/"T2" in es and vi; it is unused, but anyone who reuses it gets mismatched languages.

## Summary per language
- ja: mostly natural. The real issues are the paywall limit hard-coded as 3 (should be 5/10), the same setting named differently in onboarding and Settings, developer wording ("サーバの更新を待って"), and errors with no next step.
- en: good tone. Main problems: inconsistent capitalization (Collection, two category styles), a few literal button labels ("To review", "Go home", "Fix the place"), "memorial album", the 6-level memory scale, and a broken "tap Log in" reference.
- zh-TW: natural Taiwan vocabulary with no mainland terms. Problems: one 您, 字 used for 單字, the chunk term 組塊, a quoted state name that doesn't match its badge, and a few ungrammatical lines.
- es: accurate. Problems: Spain and Latin-American forms mixed, gendered wording ("el primero", masculine for palabra), "Ejemplo" used for two things on one page, missing articles in the legal sentence, «» used once.
- ko: 해요체 is mostly consistent. Problems: onboarding 맞춥니다, the 나에게…드려요 perspective clash, a few unnatural lines, mixed 제보/신고, particles after placeholders, and errors in the sample card.
- vi: consistent "bạn" register. One real meaning error ("Thứ tự mình chụp"); otherwise inconsistent terms (camera/máy ảnh, Cấp/Trình độ, Chạm/Nhấn).
